# Manufacturing AI workflow pilot: offer and proof

## Buyer and outcome

CXOs, directors, and IT leaders at manufacturers with $10–70M annual revenue, small teams, and little internal software capacity. Prioritize people showing first-hand AI interest on LinkedIn (posts, comments, newsletter engagement where visible) or a relevant KnowledgeNet client showcase; a logo is only a research lead, not evidence of buying intent or permission to imply an endorsement. This campaign-specific ICP does not replace other outreach funnels.

**Outcome:** choose one repetitive workflow and make a safe working trial that the customer's team can operate. Agree on a baseline before promising savings; measure the same quantity afterward (e.g. minutes per ticket, rework rate, or time to answer). Do not promise a numerical improvement without evidence.

## Free pilot and paid next step

Public application for **two companies at a time**. One workflow, 12 weeks, up to two Basalt consulting hours per company per week (24 total). Basalt finds where AI could do the most work, builds a safe working trial, and hands it over for the team to operate. Selection requires a real bottleneck, a decision-maker and internal owner willing to participate, a measurable baseline, and a safe way to test without sensitive production data. An application does not reserve a slot. Ask permission before placing anyone on a waitlist. This ICP is for internal qualification, not public page copy; do not request revenue in the form.

- Weeks 1–2: baseline, agree on success and access boundaries, build a safe proof of concept.
- Weeks 3–10: trial and improve the one workflow alongside their team; teach its owner how to operate it.
- Weeks 11–12: compare the same measure with baseline, hand off the working trial and instructions, invite candid feedback.

Production integrations, sensitive-data access, external tool charges, additional workflows, and ongoing support require separate agreement. The trial and handoff stand on their own; there is no required paid conversion or positive testimonial. Offer a separately scoped, fixed-price production implementation only when the pilot reveals that the customer wants it. Three months of Claude may be funded when tool access is the actual obstacle, but it is not an automatic benefit for new applicants. **Requests received while `/gift` advertised the unconditional three-month Claude subscription must receive that original gift and game plan.** Contact anyone personally if fulfillment fails.
